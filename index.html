Image Carve

Turn a photo into a black and white V-bit engraving. Light areas become carved grooves; shadows stay as uncut material. Everything runs in your browser. Your image is never uploaded.

Tip: for photos with a bright background and dark subjects, turn on Invert or the subjects stay as solid silhouettes.
